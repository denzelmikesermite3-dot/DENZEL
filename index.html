A special devotion

Our Lady of Candles

The cathedral is closely associated with Nuestra Señora de la Candelaria, or Our Lady of Candles, whose devotion is an important part of Catholic life in Western Visayas.

The church and its separate historic bell tower are distinctive landmarks of the Jaro district.
