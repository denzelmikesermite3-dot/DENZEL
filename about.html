About Jaro Cathedral

A sacred landmark that reflects the faith, culture, and heritage of the people of Iloilo.

A Cathedral of Faith

Jaro Metropolitan Cathedral, officially known as the Cathedral of St. Elizabeth of Hungary and the National Shrine of Our Lady of Candles, is located in Jaro, Iloilo City, Philippines.

It serves as the seat of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Jaro and is an important place of worship and pilgrimage for Catholics in Western Visayas.

The cathedral is especially associated with devotion to Nuestra Señora de la Candelaria, also known as Our Lady of Candles.

What Makes It Special?

The cathedral is both a place of religious devotion and a landmark of local cultural heritage.

Religious Significance

The shrine is an important center of devotion to Our Lady of Candles and a place for prayer, worship, and pilgrimage.

Distinctive Architecture

The cathedral is known for its Romanesque Revival architectural features, including rounded arches and a prominent facade.

Historic Bell Tower

Its separate bell tower stands across the road near Jaro Plaza and is a recognized historical landmark in its own right.

A Heritage for Future Generations

Beyond its religious role, Jaro Cathedral is part of the cultural identity of Iloilo. Its architecture, history, and traditions connect the present community with generations that came before.

Visitors are encouraged to respect the sacred nature of the church, observe any posted rules, and be mindful of people attending religious services.
